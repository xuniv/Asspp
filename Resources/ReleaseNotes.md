Downloads opens first on iOS, followed by Accounts, Tools, Settings, and Search. Downloaded apps show storefront, decrypted-copy, and update badges. Older versions are grouped under the newest version. Long-press a download to check its storefront for updates and see IPA size and decrypted-copy availability. Tap the refresh button to check all downloaded apps, or touch and hold to check and download updates with progress.

Tools contains IPA import and installation, the choice of installation domain, UnfairPlay models, and persistent processed IPAs. Asspp can generate and use a device-local CA for asspp.localhost offline. Existing installations can continue to use app.localhost.qaq.wiki. Install and enable full trust for the certificate profile of the domain you select.

Processed IPAs remain until deleted. Share, save to Files, or open a copy in Feather for signing. These builds are unsigned. The English AltStore package is also available through https://xuniv.github.io/alt/source.json.
